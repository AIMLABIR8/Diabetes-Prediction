About DiaSense AI

What is DiaSense AI?

DiaSense AI is an advanced machine learning-powered healthcare application designed to assess diabetes risk based on key health metrics. Using state-of-the-art algorithms trained on extensive medical datasets, it provides accurate, instant predictions to help users understand their health status.

How It Works

DiaSense AI analyzes 8 critical health metrics including glucose levels, blood pressure, BMI, age, and more. Our Random Forest classifier processes this data through trained models to generate a diabetes risk prediction with probability scores, giving you actionable insights in seconds.

Key Features

  • 98% Accuracy: ML-powered predictions with high precision
  • Instant Results: Get risk assessment in seconds
  • Secure & Private: Your health data is encrypted and protected
  • Personalized Recommendations: Actionable health advice based on your results
  • Real-time Analysis: FastAPI backend for quick processing
  • Beautiful UI: Modern, intuitive interface for easy use

Health Metrics Analyzed

  • Pregnancies
  • Glucose Level (mg/dL)
  • Blood Pressure (mm Hg)
  • Skin Thickness (mm)
  • Insulin Level (mu U/ml)
  • BMI (Body Mass Index)
  • Diabetes Pedigree Function
  • Age (years)
